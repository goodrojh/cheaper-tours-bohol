# Marketing Strategy — Cheaper Tours & Travel Bohol

## Niche analysis

**Market.** Bohol is one of the top-3 island destinations in the Philippines (Chocolate Hills, tarsiers, Panglao beaches, Balicasag diving). The tour market is crowded but highly commoditized: dozens of operators sell nearly identical Countryside / Island Hopping / Panglao day tours at similar prices, mostly through Facebook pages, Klook/GetYourGuide resellers, and hotel desks.

**Who buys.** Four dominant segments:
1. **Western couples & backpackers** (AU, EU, US) — value authenticity, hate tourist-bus crowds, book via WhatsApp/reviews.
2. **East Asian travelers** (KR, JP, CN, TW) — value safety, clear pricing, photo opportunities.
3. **Filipino domestic groups & families** — value per-group (not per-head) pricing.
4. **Divers & adventurers** — come specifically for Balicasag, Napaling, Danao.

**What hooks people in this niche (from review mining on similar operators):**
- The **driver-as-guide-photographer** is the single most praised thing in 5-star reviews ("he took amazing photos of us at every stop").
- **Per-group pricing** — families instantly understand the savings vs per-person tours.
- **Private = no strangers, no waiting** — the #1 complaint about cheap joiner tours.
- **Direct owner** — travelers actively search "no middleman" after being burned by agency markups and no-shows.
- **Fear removal** — instant WhatsApp reply, hotel pickup, transparent "entrance fees not included" honesty.

## USP (unique selling proposition)

> **Private Bohol tours at direct-owner prices — one price for the whole car, with a driver who is your guide and your free photographer.**

Three pillars, each answering a real objection:
1. **#DirectOwner, zero commission** → "am I overpaying an agency?"
2. **Per-group pricing** → "how much for a family of 5?"
3. **Driver = guide = photographer** → "will I get good photos / will I understand what I'm seeing?"

Supporting conversion mechanics on the site:
- WhatsApp deep links with pre-filled tour name on every CTA (2-click booking).
- Floating WhatsApp button (the #1 converting element for PH tour operators).
- Social proof: rating, traveler count, review cards.
- Urgency softener: "reply within minutes, 7 days a week" instead of fake timers.
- 9 languages targeting the actual visitor mix of Bohol (EN default + zh/ja/fr/de/it/es/hi/ar with RTL).
